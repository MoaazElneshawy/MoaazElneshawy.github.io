Privacy policy — MathRace

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to the MathRace app for Android, published by Moaaz Elneshawy (package com.beansproduction.mathrace)

MathRace is a simple arithmetic game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and I never see your name, email, phone number, or contacts. This page explains the small amount of data that is involved, why, where it goes, and how you can control it.

  • No accounts, no registration, and I collect no identifying data about you.
  • Your high score and sound setting are saved on your device only.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID and approximate location to pick and measure ads.
  • I don’t sell your data and I don’t share it with anyone for marketing.
  • If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your ad-consent choices before loading ads.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your best score, whether sound effects are on, and the language and theme you chose. This lives in the app’s private storage and is never sent to me. On Android it may be included in your phone’s encrypted Google backup, which I can’t read. Deleting the app deletes it all.

Ads (Google AdMob)

MathRace is free and funded by banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (resettable in Android settings), IP address, and coarse location derived from it.
  • Device information: model, OS version, language, screen size, and which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics for the ads SDK itself.

Google processes this data under its own terms. You can read how it uses advertising data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Ads shown to users in the game are requested as suitable for a general audience.

Your ad choices

  • Turn off ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app shows Google’s consent form on first launch; you can change your choice later from the app’s settings screen.
  • Ads cannot be removed from the app at the moment; there is no paid ad-free version.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.
  • It does not use its own analytics or crash-reporting service; the only network traffic is for ads.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites beyond what the advertising ID allows, as described above.

Children

MathRace is suitable for all ages and is not specifically aimed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children; ad requests are made with Google’s general-audience settings and no age-sensitive data is collected by the app itself. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, email me and I will help.

Data retention and deletion

I hold no data about you, so there is nothing for me to retain or delete. Data about you held by Google for advertising is governed by Google’s retention policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings linked above.

Changes to this policy

If anything material changes — for example if I add a leaderboard or analytics — I will update this page and the effective date at the top.
