MathRace

Android — free, supported by ads · Arabic and English

MathRace is a quick mental-arithmetic game: answer as many addition, subtraction, multiplication and division questions as you can before the timer runs out, use a hint when you’re stuck, and chase your own high score. I first released it in 2018 and am now rebuilding it from scratch in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, keeping your saved high score and sound settings when you update.

Download

  • Google Play — the updated version is in progress; the original 2018 release is still listed under the same app

What the app does with your data

There are no accounts and nothing to sign up for. Your high score and sound preference are stored on your device only. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which is the only data-related third party involved. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Source code
