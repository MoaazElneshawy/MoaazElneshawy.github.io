Quran Meanings معاني القرآن

Android · iOS · Huawei AppGallery — free, no ads, no account, works offline

Read the Quran in the Uthmani script with the meaning of every word shown beside the verse, a concise tafsir for each group of verses, full-text search, bookmarks, prayer times and qibla, and categorised adhkar you can share as text or image. The content is bundled inside the app, so reading works without an internet connection.

Privacy policy

Quran Meanings has no accounts, no ads, and collects no identifying data. It uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand usage and fix crashes, and uses your approximate location only if you tap “Use my location” on the prayer-times screen.
